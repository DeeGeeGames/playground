Spring Run

A gunner on the left fires across the lane. You are stuck on a mark in the middle. Drag the stick to spring out, up to the ring. Release, and you pull back.

3 lives. Drag the stick to spring off the mark. Release to return.

Spring Drag the stick, or hold W A S D / arrows. Gamepad left stick. Release to return. You cannot leave the ring.
