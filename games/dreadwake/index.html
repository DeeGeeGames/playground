BROADSIDE CREWS / TOGGLE G

Manual by default. Auto crews fire each six-gun array only with a valid side solution. Q/E still commit salvos manually. Toggling to manual stops new automatic salvos; committed salvos finish.

BOW TORPEDOES / TAP T

Three homing rounds, 14-second reload. Requires a target within 1,000 m and 60° of the bow. Priority wins when valid; otherwise the nearest bow target is used. A salvo keeps its target. Holding T does not repeat.

POINT DEFENSE / ALWAYS AUTO

Intercepts one threatening round every 0.38 seconds within 240 m, including during armor lock. It thins volleys; keep evading and using defenses.

ARMOR LOCK / HOLD SPACE

65% damage reduction. Reduces drive and reload speed. Prevents new broadsides and torpedoes; committed salvos finish.

AEGIS PULSE / TAP F

85% damage reduction for 3 seconds. 18-second cooldown. Does not slow the ship or weapons. Defensive bonuses do not stack.

FIRE CONTROL

Click or tap a hostile to prioritize it within each weapon's arc. Otherwise the nearest valid target is acquired. The weapon systems work together; there is no selection cycle.
