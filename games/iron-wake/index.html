READ THE FIELD. Orange circles mark fixed artillery impacts. Leave the marked ground. Siege frames resist frontal fire; flank them or attack during the EXPOSED recovery window. The Crown commits to larger barrages below half armor.

BUILD YOUR ARSENAL. Start with one weapon; eliminations earn weapon drafts, including during assaults. Fill four mounts, then upgrade a weapon to rank III or replace one. All weapons aim with the turret; missiles guide toward a lock, rockets fly straight. Cluster ordnance bursts over an area. PPC bolts pierce targets.

REFIT & RECOVER. Drive over salvage for repairs and coolant. Each cleared assault earns a permanent upgrade for this operation.
