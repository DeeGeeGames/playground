ORDERS HELD.

The ship follows its course automatically. Fit weapons on either flank; Full Voyage turrets cover 120° outward from their flank; Escort Drill and Systems Trial turrets cover 240°. Select a fitted mount to see its current range and arc. Beams deal sustained instant damage; armor reduces their damage. Railguns ignore armor. Missiles explode on impact.

Open Cover to allocate escort coverage to Fore, Aft, Port and Starboard. Priorities are Off, Low, Normal, High and All. All sends every available escort to one side and holds the other priorities until released. Balanced restores Normal on every side. Escorts choose positions and intercept within their sector. Fighters start docked. In Escort Bay, Scramble all fighters starts recurring sorties using radar coverage. Recall all fighters brings airborne wings home and holds them docked until scrambled again. Returning fighters cannot fire. Losses cost salvage to replace while docked or rearming. Click an enemy to prioritize eligible turrets within range and coverage; escorts defend their assigned sectors. Destroyed escorts must be replaced. Destroying every deployed hostile earns +50 salvage and repairs 60 hull. Passing a defensive line grants no clearance reward.

Ship systems and mission rules

All modes: energy shields absorb damage before hull, recharge at 4 HP/s per shield power after 3 seconds without an ark hit, and stay separate from the five-second Aegis hull protection. Aegis does not refill energy shields. Split ten reactor units among engines, weapons and shields using presets or transfer one unit between named systems. Engines control real travel at 6 m/s per unit; weapons change ark turret cadence. Full Voyage: break through six defensive lines along the 10.08 km route and reach Kepler alive. Arrival wins even with surviving hostiles; there is no minimum combat time. Defensive ships hold route positions and intercept locally, then disengage once you pass their line. More engine power brings you into the next line sooner. Escort Drill: travel 4.32 km, clear every contact and survive at least 90 seconds. Systems Trial travels 5.76 km before 155 seconds; balanced power takes about 120 seconds. In Escort Drill and Systems Trial, early arrival still waits for scheduled contacts. Stalling cannot complete the route. Both sensor bands stay active: long-range detection reaches 8 km; the short radar scale covers 2800 m, with classification inside 2560 m. T / Radar switches to a live heading-up distance display; Escape returns to battle. Threats approach through real space, with no scheduled enemy information revealed before detection. Damaged sensors detect to 4 km and classify inside 920 m. Offline sensors retain 800 m local defense. Classified bombers show a two-second ARRAY SHOT countdown before release, then FIRED and RECHARGING. Destroy them during preparation, intercept their rounds with escorts, or block them with a field. Penetrating rounds damage equipment at their actual impact location. Engines, each fitted gun, sensors and the shield generator can be impaired below 50% integrity, or offline at zero. Impaired engines and guns work at half output. Impaired generators recharge at half speed; offline generators stop recharging but stored shield charge still protects the ark. Activate Aegis after FIRED and before impact to clear flying rounds; its ongoing hull protection does not prevent sensor damage. Offline sensors retain nearby automatic defense. Four reusable repair crews are shared by hull and equipment. Each crew repairs 12 hull or 4 equipment integrity per simulation second. Reactor ± controls split or concentrate crews; E and S assign or release hull and sensor crews. Crew time is free, progress is retained on reassignment, and full repairs release crews. Repairs pause with the simulation and cannot revive the ark. Selling or upgrading a gun does not repair its mount. Interdiction / I: preview an ahead/flank field, tap the battlefield or use arrows to aim, then Enter or Place field to deploy for 90 salvage. Esc cancels the preview. One world-anchored field lasts 30 seconds, blocks hostile fire and redirects approaches; it drifts aft as the ark advances.
