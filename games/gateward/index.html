The ark is slow. The enemy is not.
Turn a capital ship into a moving fortress and survive the passage to Kepler Gate.

  1. BUILDFit eight mounts with flak, railguns, and missiles. Earn salvage from every kill.

COMMAND LINK / STANDBY

ORDERS HELD.

The ship follows its course automatically. Fit weapons on either flank; voyage turrets have full arcs; drill turrets cover 240° outward from their flank. Railguns ignore armor. Missiles explode on impact.

In the voyage, click empty space to rally escorts. In the drill, use each wing’s sector scramble and each guardian’s sector buttons; fighters return and rearm, and losses cost salvage to replace. Click an enemy to prioritize eligible turrets within range and coverage; voyage escorts also honor nearby priorities. Drill escorts defend their assigned sectors. Destroyed escorts must be replaced. Clearing a wave earns +50 salvage and repairs 60 hull.
