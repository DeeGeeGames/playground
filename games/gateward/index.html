COMMAND LINK / STANDBY

ORDERS HELD.

Scout → fit and arrange → launch → automatic combat → refit. Three 45-second passages lead to Kepler. Passage exits leave survivors behind. Fitting freezes time and resources; guns can only be bought, sold or upgraded there. Start with 650 salvage. Full refunds include upgrades. Between passages: +200 salvage, +120 hull, equipment and chassis restored. Destroyed platform guns are lost.

Ark guns cover their flank. Ships have one, two or four mounts facing outward from their formation position, with real range and 120° arcs. Exposed ships draw hostile fire and have no shields. Choose a mount to see coverage. Formation selections swap occupied slots. Click a ring position to construct a hull, or a numbered mount to fit, upgrade or salvage weapons in place. Select a platform, choose Move / swap, then click its destination. Click the ark for repair, Aegis and escort orders; advanced systems remain in drawers.

Ship systems

Convoy cruise speed is fixed: engines cannot stall a passage. Weapons and shields still use reactor power. Shields protect only the ark. Repairs, escort orders, radar and interdiction remain optional. Sensors classify contacts; damaged equipment reduces output. Time, repair and recharge freeze between passages, while paused or without focus. At a passage exit equipment and platform chassis recover automatically. Use the playfield or Fit drawer to construct ships and fit mounts, then launch the next passage.
