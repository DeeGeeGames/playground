Controls & field manual

Build Select a mount → fit a weapon. Select a ship → move or swap formation. Construction and fitting are available between passages, with full weapon refunds.

Battle Guns fire automatically. Click a hostile to focus eligible weapons. Survive to the exit. Q Aegis clears rounds and protects the ark for 5 seconds. E assigns a hull repair crew.

Keyboard Space pause · F fit · T radar · C escort coverage · L launch · 1–8 ark mounts · Esc back. Tab and Enter operate menus.

Camera Drag to orbit. Wheel or pinch to zoom. Select a ship and Follow ship, or use Fleet. Reset view returns to the ark.

Controller Right stick orbits · triggers zoom · Y opens a drawer · bumpers change drawers · D-pad navigates or adjusts · A activates · B returns · Start pauses.

Refit Each safe passage grants 200 salvage and up to 120 ark hull. Chassis and equipment recover; destroyed platform weapons must be replaced. Choose one optional support package before continuing.

Advanced commands Power divides the reactor between weapons and shields. Bay manages escorts; Cover assigns their sectors. Intel monitors sensors. Shields and repair crews protect the ark; platforms rely on their position and guns.

Current progress will be lost. Select the same action again to confirm, or return to command to keep playing.
